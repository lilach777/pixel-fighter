# Move List & Frame Data

_Generated by `scripts/generate-docs.ts` from `shared/src/characters/roster.ts`. Do not edit by hand._

Frame data is in 60 Hz frames. **Startup** = frame the first hitbox appears; **Active** = frames the hitbox is out; **Recovery** = frames after. Damage is out of the character's health (≈1000). Multi-hit moves split damage evenly. Combo damage scaling: 100%, 100%, 85%, 72%, 62%, 52%, 45%, 40%, 35%, then 30%.

Levels: **high** whiffs on crouchers' shorter hurtbox, **mid** blocks either way, **low** must be crouch-blocked, **overhead** must be stand-blocked.

These are fictional gameplay designs inspired by the sprite artwork, not statements about real people.
